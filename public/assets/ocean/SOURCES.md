# Ocean materials

Downloaded 2026-09-06 from Poly Haven's asset API, 1K JPEG maps.

- Sand 01: https://polyhaven.com/a/sand_01
- Coast Sand Rocks 02: https://polyhaven.com/a/coast_sand_rocks_02
- License: CC0, https://polyhaven.com/license

Each material includes Diffuse (sRGB), nor_gl (OpenGL normal), and rough (linear roughness) maps.
These are original texture files; the game applies its own underwater tint and lighting.
Water ceiling and animated light patterns are procedural project code, not downloaded photographs.

Other free libraries reviewed: https://ambientcg.com and https://www.cgbookcase.com.
No assets from those libraries are bundled in this directory.
